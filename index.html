Cast Biography

❦ ❦ ❦

PICKLES is thrilled to make her theatrical debut in Anastasia.

This marks her first production. She has previously appeared in no other musicals, as she is a dog who has only recently emerged from a period of mourning. She is currently navigating life as a widow dog following the loss of her late boyfriend, Caesar.

Interests & Off-Stage Pursuits

❦ ❦ ❦

When not in rehearsal, Pickles maintains a full and varied personal schedule. She approaches her leisure time with the same commitment she brings to the stage.

Waste Management ResearchPickles conducts independent, hands-on audits of household refuse. Her preferred method begins with the controlled toppling of the kitchen waste receptacle, followed by a thorough, unhurried review of its contents, most of which are consumed on site.
Culinary DevelopmentA devoted student of the arts of acquisition, Pickles is always open to negotiating a snack. Her signature technique is the sustained stare, delivered at close range.
Restorative PracticePickles observes a rigorous schedule of blanket-based rest, often adopting a thoughtful side profile suitable for portraiture.
Perimeter ObservationShe keeps a watchful eye on the front window and monitors all passersby, squirrels, and deliveries in dignified silence.
